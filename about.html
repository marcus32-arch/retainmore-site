About

Automation you can actually rely on, run by someone who answers the phone.

Not a marketing agency that bolted AI onto its pitch deck. A practical way to handle the part of running a clinic that nobody has time for.

Most "AI for clinics" pitches lead with the AI. We lead with the result: fewer empty appointment slots, fewer patients who quietly stop coming back, and a front desk that isn't spending its day chasing people by phone.

This industry is regulated, and you should hire like it is

RetainMore is run by a biomedical engineer who has worked in the medical technology industry and consulted with clinics, which means the rules you operate under are not a surprise to us. How sensitive patient information has to be stored and handled. What AHPRA lets you say in anything sent in your clinic's name, and what it doesn't. Where the line sits between scheduling follow-up and making a clinical call. These constraints shaped how the system was designed, they were not discovered after it was built.

The fastest way to feel cheated in this market is to hire someone confident who doesn't know your industry, and to find out when it matters. Our answer to that is the compliance page: the obligations you carry, how our builds respect them, in writing, before you sign anything. Put the same questions to anyone else quoting you.

Why every message follows a fixed rule

If a patient misses an appointment, a message goes out. Every time, the same way, on the same schedule, whether it's their first missed visit or their fifth. Nothing is left to guesswork about what a patient should be told or when. That reliability is also why the cost stays predictable as your clinic grows: you're not paying more per message as your patient list gets bigger.

AI still has a place in this business. It drafts the first reply to a new website enquiry so nobody waits until Monday, and it runs the periodic marketing check-in. Both are bounded uses that a person can review, not something deciding what a patient hears every single day.

Why we check first, before we build anything

Cliniko, Nookal, and Halaxy each already do some of this natively. Before quoting anyone, we check exactly what your specific system already covers, and just as often, whether it's actually switched on and being used. A lot of clinics are paying for automation that's sitting half configured. That check is a one-time cost, bundled into the setup fee. It's never used to justify the monthly retainer, because ongoing monitoring is the only thing that's actually ongoing.

What working with us looks like

One point of contact for the build and for anything that needs fixing afterward, not a support queue. Workflows are tested against your own real appointment data before they go live, not launched blind. The retainer is month to month. If it stops earning its keep for your clinic, you're not locked into it.
