The problem

Your booking system handles the appointment. Nothing handles what happens next.

Cliniko, Nookal, and Halaxy are good at booking. What they leave to your front desk, chasing no-shows, recalling lapsed patients, answering new enquiries fast, is where the revenue actually leaks, and it's exactly the part that's hardest to staff for.

No-shows don't come back on their own

A missed appointment is a lost session and an empty slot nobody refilled. Every no-show triggers a follow-up within the hour, automatically, without anyone at your front desk remembering to make the call.

Slow replies lose new patients

A prospective patient fills in a web form on Thursday afternoon. If reception doesn't see it until Monday, they have usually booked somewhere that answered first. A fast reply is often the difference.

Lapsed patients need a reason to return

Your patient list is full of people who came once or twice and stopped. Almost none of them announced they were leaving. They got busy, and nobody asked them back. We ask, in your name, at the point their own history says they are most likely to answer.

Your team's time goes back to patients

Manually phoning no-shows and typing reminder texts is real staff time. Automating it frees your front desk to spend that time with the people already in the building.

Fewer patients falling through the cracks with Medicare

A GP Chronic Condition Management Plan covers five allied health visits a calendar year, shared across every discipline the patient sees, and it resets on 1 January. Referrals expire on their own clock. Patients often stop coming because they didn't realise they needed a new one, not because they didn't want to. We track it and prompt them before it lapses.

Your booking system reports on operations: bookings, invoices, utilisation. Nothing shows you retention and growth in one place. We build that view so you can see what's working.

Patients with a habit of missing appointments get more reminders. Reliable patients get fewer. Nobody gets over-messaged, and it sits on top of whatever your PMS already sends.
