Why RetainMore, not another app

Cliniko, Nookal, and Halaxy already do some of this. Here's what none of them do.

We checked. Reminders and basic no-show follow-up already exist inside most booking systems, and we won't sell you something you're already paying for. These four don't exist anywhere in your practice management software, whichever one you use.

01

Fewer patients falling through the cracks with Medicare

A GP Chronic Condition Management Plan covers five allied health visits a calendar year, shared across every discipline the patient sees, and it resets on 1 January. Referrals expire on their own clock. Patients often stop coming because they didn't realise they needed a new one, not because they didn't want to. We track it and prompt them before it lapses.

02

One clear view of how your clinic is actually growing

Your booking system reports on operations: bookings, invoices, utilisation. Nothing shows you retention and growth in one place. We build that view so you can see what's working.

03

Messaging your patients won't find annoying

Patients with a habit of missing appointments get more reminders. Reliable patients get fewer. Nobody gets over-messaged, and it sits on top of whatever your PMS already sends.

04

Someone accountable for it working, not one more login

We build it, we monitor it, and we're responsible for it working. Not a self-serve dashboard you have to configure and keep an eye on yourself.

How it works

Automations that run while you're with patients.

Reliable, predictable follow-up connected directly to your booking system. Nothing unpredictable making decisions about your patients, and no costs that climb as your clinic grows.

Workflow

Appointment Reminders

Reminders at 72 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours by SMS and email, with a simple confirm or cancel link, timed to what each booking system allows.

Workflow

No-Show Recovery

An SMS goes out within 30 minutes of a marked no-show, an email with a direct rebook link follows at 24 hours, and a final nudge or receptionist flag at 7 days.

Workflow

New Enquiry Response

A web form submission gets an immediate, personal reply with a booking link. Not a Monday-morning callback to someone who's already booked elsewhere.
