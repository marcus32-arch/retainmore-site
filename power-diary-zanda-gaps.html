For practices running Power Diary, now Zanda

Booked out is not the same as on top of it.

Your calendar is full, your day is wall to wall, and the practice still needs a receptionist, a bookkeeper and a marketer. Right now all three are you, after the last session, at the kitchen table.

Where it leaks

The practice runs on your evenings.

  • The enquiry lands at 7:40 pm on a Tuesday. You're with clients all day Wednesday, you reply Thursday night, and they booked Wednesday morning with whoever answered first. You never even got to lose them in person. That's not a marketing problem, it's a speed problem, and it repeats every week you're good enough to be busy.
  • The follow-up list lives in your head. The client who stopped booking, the one who said they'd be back after school holidays, the reports promised for Friday. Chasing any of it loses to the person in front of you every time, so it waits for the evening, and the evening has a queue.
  • The growth ceiling is your admin hours. Every new client adds correspondence, agreements and follow-up, and all of it routes through you. So the practice grows exactly as fast as your capacity to absorb unpaid work at night, which is the one resource you can't buy more of.

None of this shows up in the calendar. The calendar looks great. It shows up in the enquiry that went quiet, the client nobody chased, and the fact that the practice's busiest unpaid employee is its owner.

What changes

The practice keeps running while you're in session.

Now picture the same Tuesday. The enquiry lands at 7:40 pm and gets a personal reply at 7:40 pm, in your wording, with your booking link. The back and forth happens while you're at dinner. When you open the calendar on Wednesday, they're in it.

The client who quietly stopped booking gets a warm nudge without you carrying the list in your head. The follow-ups fire on their own schedule. And once a month you read a single page: what went out, what came back, what booked, what needs you. That's the entire admin.

What that buys is simple to say. The book fills from enquiries you never had to catch. The people who drifted come back before the gap they leave reaches your revenue. And the hours after dinner go back to being yours, which is the part you'll actually feel first.

  • One new client covers most of it. Check the arithmetic against your own consult fee and what a typical client is worth across their care. A single enquiry a month that books because it was answered first is the bar this has to clear.
  • Built around the system you already run. Connected to Zanda. Nothing to migrate, no new login for you or your team, nothing new to learn.
  • Your wording, approved once. Every message that goes out is one you signed off up front. Month to month, no lock-in, and the templates stay yours if you leave.
  • Watched by us, not by you. If something breaks, we find it and fix it at our cost, and you hear it from us first. You should never be the monitoring.
