Worth it or not

Do the arithmetic once.

Say the admin runs six hours a week, and your clinical hour bills at $180. That's $1,080 a week of clinician time going to work that pays nothing, most of it after hours. Across a year, the better part of $50,000. And with a waitlist, those hours were never spare time. They were sessions someone was waiting for.

Your numbers will be different. Run your own in the calculator, or bring them to the call and we'll work through where the hours are actually going.
