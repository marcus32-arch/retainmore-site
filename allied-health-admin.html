Being straight with you

What we're not.

We're not your auditor and we're not a compliance consultancy. We don't write your policies, interpret a standard for you, or tell you you'll pass. That's judgement work, it belongs with someone qualified to carry it, and anyone promising it to a practice they've never seen is selling you something.

What we do is make sure that when that person arrives, or an auditor does, the evidence is already there and current. The recurring cost was never the judgement. It was the gathering.
