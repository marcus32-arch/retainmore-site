FAQ

The questions clinic owners actually ask.

Straight answers, including the ones that occasionally talk someone out of buying. Those are the ones worth reading.

Doesn't my booking system already do this?+
Some of it, yes, and we will tell you which parts. Reminders and basic no-show follow-up exist in most systems. Having a feature available and having it configured, monitored and actually working are different things, and most clinics have exactly that sitting half-used. The first thing we do is an audit of what your system already covers, and you hear the honest answer before any invoice.
Is this AI sending messages to my patients?+
No. Every message a patient receives comes from a fixed template you approved, sent on rules you can read. We use AI heavily to build and audit your setup, but nothing unpredictable writes to a patient or makes decisions about one. That is a design rule, not a preference.
Which booking systems do you work with?+
All the majors: Cliniko, Nookal, Halaxy, Zanda, Splose and PracSuite among them, and the list is not closed. How deep the automation goes depends on what your system exposes, which is exactly what the audit checks. And the first pillar, answering your web enquiries instantly, does not depend on your booking system at all. Whatever you run, you are not disqualified, you are one audit away from knowing.
What happens on the 15-minute call?+
We go through which follow-up gaps are real for your specific booking system, and which ones it already covers. You leave with the list either way, whether or not you work with us.
What does it cost, and am I locked in?+
A one-time setup that covers the audit and the build, then a monthly retainer for monitoring and maintenance, month to month. No lock-in, and the message templates stay yours if you leave. Current figures are on the pricing page.
How long until it's running?+
The Starter flagship, new-enquiry response, is usually live inside a couple of weeks of getting access. Larger builds are scoped on the call before anything is promised.
Do you replace my practice management software?+
No. We connect to the system you already run through its official interface and add the follow-up layer around it. No migration, no new patient database, and no new login for your team to check.
Can you track Medicare care plan visits?+
A GP Chronic Condition Management Plan covers five allied health visits a calendar year, shared across disciplines, resetting on 1 January, and referrals expire on their own clock. We track this and prompt patients before it lapses. It is confirmed buildable on Cliniko and Halaxy; on other systems the audit confirms what their interface exposes before we promise it.
Who sees my patient data?+
It stays in your booking system. We connect through access you grant and can revoke, records are not copied into marketing tools or enriched or sold, and messages go only to your own patients about their own care. The compliance page covers this in full.
Won't my patients find the messages annoying?+
The cadence adapts to each patient's own attendance history. Patients who reliably show up get fewer messages, patients with a habit of missing get more, and every message carries an opt-out that is honoured immediately and permanently.

Got one we didn't answer?

Ask it on the call. 15 minutes, and the hard ones are welcome.
