What happens in Cliniko when someone cancels?

The appointment comes off the calendar, and the hour becomes nobody's job. It's 9:40 on a Tuesday, this afternoon's 4:15 just cancelled by text, and the only person who could chase it is also checking in a queue of patients with a phone that won't stop. There's a whole day to fill that slot. Watch it disappear anyway.

Appointments Tuesday, today
3:30 pm Sam W. Standard consult
4:15 pm Daniel R. Cancelled 9:40 am, by text
5:00 pm Priya K. Review

Six hours of notice. The counter had other plans, all of them.

The real cost

The hour was fillable. The window wasn't.

  • Filling it is a job with five steps and no spare minute. Notice the cancellation. Work out who'd actually take a 4:15 this afternoon. Ring the first person, get voicemail, ring the next. All of it from a front desk that's also greeting patients, taking payments and answering the phone. The chase loses to the counter every time, not because anyone is slack, but because the counter is the job.
  • A clinic hour doesn't keep. Stock that doesn't sell today sells tomorrow. A Tuesday 4:15 that goes unfilled is just gone, and the practitioner was paid for it either way. Three of those a week at a $105 consult is around $1,300 a month, quietly, forever. Run your own numbers in the calculator.
  • Sometimes it's the last you see of them. A cancellation that never gets rebooked isn't always one lost appointment. For the patient partway through a care plan, it can be the day they quietly drop off, and the rest of their course of care goes with them.
What changes

Filled between patients, by nobody.

Same Tuesday. The cancellation lands at 9:40, and by 9:42 the patients who said they'd take an earlier time have the offer in their hand, in your wording, with a way to claim it that doesn't need a phone call back. First yes takes the slot. The desk never stopped doing its actual job, and by 10:15 this afternoon's 4:15 is a booking again.

4:15 pmDaniel R. Today Cancelled 9:40 am
Hi Liam, a 4:15 this afternoon has just opened up with Sarah. You asked us for an earlier time. Reply YES and it is yours.
Sent · 9:42 am
YES. See you then.
Slot filled · Liam C. 10:15 am

Run that forward a quarter and it compounds. The mid-morning gaps stop leaking, the patients who wanted earlier appointments start getting them, and the person who used to do the ringing gets that slice of every day back. The book holds its shape without anyone holding it.

  • Your wording, approved once. You sign off the messages up front, not one by one. What goes out is what you approved, and we keep the approval on file.
  • Your rules on who gets offered what. Ordered by fit to the slot and who asked first. Never by anything clinical, ever.
  • You still hold the calendar. The system does the chasing and hands you the yes. Nothing books itself without you.
  • When something breaks, that's ours. Connected to Cliniko, watched by us. If it stops, we find it and fix it at our cost, and you hear it from us first.
